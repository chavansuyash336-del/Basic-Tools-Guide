🔧 Basic Tools Guide

Learn about common tools and their uses

🔨 Hammer

A hammer is used for driving nails, breaking materials and general construction work.

🪛 Screwdriver

A screwdriver is used to tighten or loosen screws.

🔧 Pliers

Pliers are used for gripping, bending and cutting wires.

🔩 Wrench

A wrench is used for tightening and loosening nuts and bolts.
